# Changelog

## 1.1 (November 2026)

- The panel and the companion move together. Carry the bird and the panel comes along; drag the panel by the bar at its top and the bird comes along.
- Let the bird go away from the edge and it hangs in the air for a moment, then glides back. With the panel open, the panel switches to the bird's other side when the bird crosses the screen.
- Every edge and corner of the panel resizes it, the top edge included, with a wider area to grab. The bird stays put while you resize. Settings > General > Panel size has Width and Height sliders, and the panel follows them as you drag.
- Paste or drop pictures into a note. They are shrunk to fit the note and saved small, and they travel with Export, Import and the daily backups.
- ⌘F inside a note finds in that note (⌘G for the next match, Esc to close). A magnifying glass in the note's top bar does the same.
- Share a note from the share button in its top bar (Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Notes and more, with its pictures), or with Share… on a note's right-click menu.
- Clicking Notes in the tab bar while a note is open goes back to the list of notes.
- Report a Bug… in Settings > General and the menu bar menu opens a GitHub issue with your Mac and Remi versions filled in.
- Fixed: clicking the companion just as the panel was closing could leave the panel open but invisible, so the next click seemed to do nothing.
- A task's Add Link… opens a link field in the row, so the link becomes the task's action instead of a saved link. Typing a task with a URL in it still works too.
- Links show a delete button on hover, like tasks, and the button turns red under the pointer.
- Clicking anywhere outside a task or link you are renaming finishes the rename.

## 1.0 (November 2026)

The first release.

- Tasks, notes and links in one panel, grouped into Spaces, opened with ⌃Space.
- Capture in one line: `#space`, dates like `tomorrow` or `fri`, `!` for important, `every monday` to repeat, and links that keep their titles.
- ⌃⌥S saves the tab you are on in Dia, Arc, Chrome, Brave, Edge or Safari.
- A companion on the edge of the screen, Perched or On the cord, in four plumages. Perched comes in four sizes, from Mini to Large.
- Search across everything, Markdown notes, reminders, Recently Deleted for 30 days.
- Daily backups, and Export and Import as JSON with a Markdown copy.
- Check for Updates opens the releases page. Remi itself never contacts a server about versions.
