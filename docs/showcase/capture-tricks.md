# Remi: capture tricks

For a case study or video showcase. Everything here happens in the capture bar at the bottom of the panel, in one line, with no forms or menus.

## The tricks

| Type | What happens |
|---|---|
| `#work` | Files it in that Space |
| `#clients` (a new name) | Creates the Space and files it there |
| `today`, `tomorrow`, `tmrw` | Sets the due date, anywhere in the line |
| `fri`, `monday` | Sets the next such day, as the last word only, so "Sat exam prep" stays a title |
| `!` | Marks it important, on its own or at the end of the last word |
| `every monday`, `every day`, `every weekday`, `every week`, `every month` | Makes it repeat |
| `daily`, `weekdays`, `weekly`, `monthly` | Makes it repeat, as the last word only |
| A link on its own | Saves to Links, with its title and icon. Videos go to Watch later |
| Tab, on a link | Switches between Saved links and Watch later before saving |
| Words plus a link | A task with a button that opens that link |
| Return | Saves as a task |
| ⌘Return | Saves the same line as a note |
| ⌘Z, with the bar empty | Undoes the last change |

Outside the capture bar: ⌃Space opens Remi from anywhere, and ⌃⌥S saves the current browser tab without opening anything.

## Good lines to type on camera

Each one shows a different trick, and the tokens above the bar fill in as you type.

1. `Send invoice #work fri !`: Space, date and importance in one breath.
2. `Stretch every day`: a repeating task, shown with a small ↻.
3. `Call the accountant #taxes tomorrow`: a Space created on the spot.
4. Paste a YouTube link: it lands in Watch later with its real title.
5. `Update my profile https://example.com/me today`: a task that is also a doorway.
6. `Ideas for the launch` then ⌘Return: the same bar makes a note.

## Moments worth filming

- The pointer comes near and Remi wakes, slides out and turns to look at you.
- A task is added: Remi hops and the egg's number goes up. On the cord, a knot ties itself on.
- ⌃⌥S in a browser: Remi pecks at the egg and a gold ring pulses out, with no panel opened.
- Pulling the cord down until the panel opens, then the bounce on release.
- The last task of the day: a check replaces the number on the egg.
- The morning review: "Good morning · 3 left from before", then Fresh start.

## The idea to open with

Sankofa: it is not wrong to go back for what you have forgotten. Remi carries what you meant to do on its back, and looks back at it.
