The idea

It is not wrong to go back for what you have forgotten.

That is the meaning of Sankofa, an Adinkra symbol of the Akan people of Ghana: a bird looking back at the egg on its back. Remi carries what you meant to do and keeps looking back at it, so you can stop holding it all in your head.

It is kind about undone things. There are no streaks and no guilt. Yesterday's tasks wait for you in the morning, and you decide what comes along.

Capture

Say it all in one line.

One bar takes everything. Type the way you think and Remi works out the Space, the date and whether it repeats. No forms and no menus.

This is a small imitation of the capture bar, so you can feel it before you install. Press Return and watch the bird.

The companion

Someone to keep the list with you.

Remi lives on the edge of your screen. It wakes when your pointer comes near, turns to look at you and carries today's count on its egg. Click it to open your list.

Perched

On a gold peg at the side of the screen, like the one on this page. It tucks into the edge when you are busy and slides out when you come back.

A new taskA hop, and the number on the egg goes up.
A link caughtIt pecks at the egg and a gold ring pulses out.
All keptThe last task of the day is done and a check replaces the number.

On the cord

A braided cord hangs from the top of the screen with Remi on a swing at the end. It ties one knot for each task due today. Pull the cord to open your list, and watch a knot come loose each time you finish something.

One place

Tasks, notes and links, together.

Three small tools that usually live in three apps. Group them into Spaces like Work and Personal, and search across all of them at once.

Private by design

Everything stays on your Mac.

There is no account and nothing to sign up for. The only time Remi reaches the internet is to fetch the title and icon of a link you save. This page does not track you either.

Your library is a file

It lives in your own Library folder, where you can see it and back it up.

Daily backups

Remi keeps the last 14 days of backups next to your library, one click away in Settings.

Leave whenever you like

Export everything as JSON and as plain Markdown. Deleted items wait 30 days before they go.

Install

Three steps, one of them unusual.

Remi is free, so it is not notarized by Apple, which needs a paid developer account. macOS will block the first open and ask you to confirm. It only happens once.

  1. Download and move it

    Unzip the download and move Remi.app to your Applications folder.

  2. Open it once

    macOS says it cannot check the app. Close that message.

  3. Allow it

    Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Remi. Confirm, and you are in.

Download for Mac

Needs macOS 26 or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Would you rather build it yourself?

You need Xcode and XcodeGen. No Apple account is needed. The script builds Remi, puts it in Applications and opens it.

brew install xcodegen
git clone https://github.com/sharrygold/remi.git
cd remi
